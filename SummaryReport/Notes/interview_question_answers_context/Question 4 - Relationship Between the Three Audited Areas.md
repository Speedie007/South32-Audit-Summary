## Contextual Interpretation

The three audited areas should be treated as **separate technical concerns within one overall audit engagement**.

### 1. Main Shaft Head Sheaves

The Main Shaft Head Sheave assessment is a standalone condition assessment focused on the **condition of the sheave groove profile**.

It forms part of the mine's required periodic inspection activities associated with safety requirements. Its purpose and interpretation are therefore distinct from both the cable belt assessment and the spillage investigation.

### 2. Cable Belt Sheaves

The Cable Belt Sheave assessment is a separate condition-assessment category within the conveyor transport system.

Three individual cable belt sheave sections were inspected and reported separately:

- `CompletedReports/ISS_IRS32-Wessels-180726 - Cable Belt Main Drive SheavesV1.4- Final.pdf`
- `CompletedReports/ISS_IRS32-Wessels-200726 - Cable Belt Angle Station SheavesV1.1-Final.pdf`
- `CompletedReports/South32_IRS32-Wessels-180726_1-Upper Gantry Guide Sheaves - Final.pdf`

Although each section was inspected and documented independently, the three groups of sheaves form part of the supporting sheave infrastructure of the cable belt system. The summary report may therefore interpret them collectively at system level where appropriate, while preserving the section-specific detail in the individual reports.

### 3. Product Spillage

The Product Spillage investigation is the third independent audit category.

It addresses the loss or escape of material/product from the conveyor transport system at South32 Wessels Mine. The detailed findings are contained in:

`CompletedReports/South32_Spillage_Audit_Report_July2026_S32WPPSR_01_FinalV1.5.pdf`

The summary report should provide only the necessary high-level context and interpretation, referring to the source report for detailed evidence.

### Relationship Between Cable Belt Sheaves and Product Spillage

The Cable Belt Sheave condition findings and the Product Spillage findings are **separate concerns**.

The summary report should not imply a technical, causal, or remedial relationship between the two unless later source evidence explicitly establishes one.

## Contextual Significance

The accepted structural model is:

- **Main Shaft Head Sheaves** — standalone periodic safety-related groove-profile condition assessment.
- **Cable Belt Sheaves** — standalone cable-belt-system condition assessment comprising three independently inspected sheave sections that may be interpreted collectively at system level.
- **Product Spillage** — standalone operational/system investigation into escaped product from the conveyor transport system.

All three belong under the same overall audit narrative, but they should remain technically independent in interpretation unless explicit evidence supports a relationship.
