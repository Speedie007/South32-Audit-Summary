## Contextual Interpretation

The Product Spillage summary should state the report's central interpretation principle prominently near the beginning of the section.

The accepted principle is:

> **The location where material accumulates is not necessarily the location where the initiating loss mechanism occurs.**

The summary should therefore avoid treating every visible deposit as proof of a local defect at the deposit location.

It should also preserve the related housekeeping principle:

> **Housekeeping addresses the accumulated consequence; source control aims to reduce recurrence.**

## Summary-Level Meaning

The summary should explain that:

- fines/carryback may remain on the return belt after discharge and detach progressively downstream;
- rollers/idlers can redistribute adhered material;
- water can mobilize fines away from an original escape point;
- belt tracking or alignment can reduce carrying margin before material is lost at another location;
- trip/restart handling can create temporary deposits that are operationally different from chronic mechanical spillage;
- a recently cleaned area demonstrates removal of the consequence, not necessarily correction of the initiating mechanism.

## Contextual Significance

This principle should appear before the main CV09 and W10 remedial narratives because it governs how the reader interprets the complete spillage section.

It prevents:

- over-attributing deposits to the location where they are found;
- assuming one common cause for all spillage;
- treating housekeeping alone as a sustainable engineering solution;
- confusing consequence management with source correction.

The wording should remain consistent with the detailed report's causal and evidence boundaries.
