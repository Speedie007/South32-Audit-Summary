## Contextual Interpretation

The coarse/large-product side-containment programme should remain selective rather than universal, but the summary should give stronger early emphasis to the known high-impact locations.

### Early Side-Skirt / Spill-Seal Implementation Group

The current intended early implementation group is:

- **W10** — primary proof-of-concept / demonstration location;
- **Sample Conveyor 2** — explicit side-skirt/spill-seal implementation candidate after prerequisite feed/loading/belt-position verification and correction;
- **Sample Conveyor 3** — explicit side-skirt/spill-seal implementation candidate after prerequisite feed/loading/tracking/alignment verification and correction.

The user wishes the Sample Conveyor applications to be presented more explicitly as part of the early remedial programme, rather than being perceived as distant or optional future applications.

W10 remains the principal demonstration/reference application because the detailed report specifically identifies it as the preferred proof location for the side-skirt/spill-seal principle.

## Source-Alignment Boundary

The detailed report supports the side-skirt/spill-seal principle at W10 and Sample Conveyors 2 and 3.

However, the Sample Conveyor installations remain conditional on first quantifying and addressing the relevant feed, product-size, loading, belt-position/tracking/alignment and local geometry contributors.

Accordingly, the summary may group W10 and Sample Conveyors 2 and 3 as the **early high-impact side-containment workstream**, but should not imply that the Sample Conveyor skirt geometry can be finalized before those prerequisite conditions are verified.

## Full Conveyor Matrix and Future Candidate Identification

The complete inspection matrix should cover the full inspected underground-to-surface conveyor/process route.

For side-skirt applicability, use distinct evidence-status categories such as:

1. **Explicitly recommended / early implementation**
2. **Report-supported future applicability**
3. **Candidate for site review**
4. **Monitor / review**
5. **Not presently indicated**

Where the detailed spillage report does not contain enough evidence to identify a conveyor as a definite skirting location, the summary must not invent a recommendation.

Instead, the matrix may identify that location as **candidate for site review** or leave the future-skirt status open for user/site confirmation.

The user may subsequently review the complete matrix and manually identify additional locations based on site knowledge, operating history or management intent. Those additions should be preserved as later user-provided contextual refinements and clearly distinguished from recommendations directly supported by the original spillage audit.

## Contextual Significance

This approach serves two purposes:

- it makes the early high-impact side-containment programme more visible;
- it creates a practical system-wide planning tool without overstating what the original audit proved.

The intended programme is therefore:

**W10 demonstration + early Sample Conveyor 2/3 implementation after prerequisite correction → verify representative performance → identify and adapt the same containment principle at other suitable locations where recurring lateral coarse-product loss and geometry justify it.**
