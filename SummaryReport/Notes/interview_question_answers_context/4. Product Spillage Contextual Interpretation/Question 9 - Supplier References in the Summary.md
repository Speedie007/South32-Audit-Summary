## Contextual Interpretation

Supplier and vendor names should generally be omitted from the high-level Product Spillage summary.

The summary should focus on the required engineering function and remedial philosophy rather than procurement detail.

Examples include:

- preferred dual-stage cleaner/scraper function with spring-loaded/self-tensioning wear compensation;
- minimum single effective tensioned cleaner stage where verified geometry prevents two stages;
- belt-compatible side-skirt/spill-seal containment where lateral coarse-product loss requires it.

Indicative suppliers, specific product references, OEM options, component details and procurement considerations should remain in the detailed Product Spillage report unless later inclusion adds clear management value.

## Contextual Significance

This keeps the summary:

- technically focused;
- vendor-neutral;
- management-readable;
- consistent with the report-level role established in the contextual foundation;
- appropriately separated from detailed design and procurement decisions.
