## Preliminary Source-Structure Understanding

The source set clearly distinguishes:

- evidence from interpretation;
- recurring/system-level mechanisms from location-specific mechanisms;
- source control from secondary containment and housekeeping/consequence management;
- functional engineering standards from identical-hardware prescriptions;
- report-level concepts from detailed-design/OEM/competent-authority responsibilities;
- completed work from demonstrated functional success under representative operation.

The final report is deliberately built around a connected material/process containment narrative rather than an asset-by-asset list alone.

These are preliminary source-understanding points only. Their role, emphasis and wording in the new audit summary remain subject to the user's overview and the Product Spillage interview round.
