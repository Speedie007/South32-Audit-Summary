## Contextual Interpretation

The complete Product Spillage inspection matrix should be placed in the **main body of the Product Spillage section**.

It should appear early enough to give management an immediate system-wide view before the narrative expands into the principal remedial themes.

The matrix should not be relegated to an appendix, because its purpose is to help the reader understand the overall audited conveyor/process system and the relationship between the principal findings and remedial philosophies.

## Contextual Significance

The matrix is a core summary-report communication tool, not supplementary reference material.

Its position in the main body reinforces the purpose of the summary report:

- present the complete inspected system at a glance;
- show the distinction between fines control and localized coarse-product containment;
- provide concise remedial-status visibility;
- direct the reader to the detailed report for evidence and implementation detail.
