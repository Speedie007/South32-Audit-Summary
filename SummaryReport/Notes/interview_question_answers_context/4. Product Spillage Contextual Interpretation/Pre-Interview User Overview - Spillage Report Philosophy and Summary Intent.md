## Contextual Interpretation

The user's intended Product Spillage summary philosophy is not to reproduce the detailed 116-page report asset by asset.

The high-level summary should instead preserve the report's system logic:

1. **Inspection coverage:** make clear that the required underground and surface conveyor/process sections were inspected, and include a complete list of the inspected conveyor sections/assets for management reference.
2. **Primary system-wide spillage theme:** fines/carryback were the dominant recurring spillage form across the conveyor ecosystem.
3. **Secondary/localized spillage theme:** larger/coarse product escape was more localized and should be treated according to local conditions rather than as a universal system-wide mechanism.
4. **Highest-impact first:** the detailed report deliberately highlighted selected locations where early remedial action could demonstrate the greatest practical effect.
5. **Fines-control pilot:** CV09 is the principal pilot/reference location for proving the cleaner-and-containment philosophy.
6. **Fines wider rollout:** after the CV09 function is demonstrated under representative operation, the cleaner-and-containment functional standard should be explicitly extended through systematic review and progressive implementation at relevant underground and surface conveyor head/discharge locations.
7. **Large-product containment pilot:** W10 is the key proof-of-concept location for the belt-compatible side-skirt/spill-seal approach because significant lateral coarse/mixed-product loss was observed there.
8. **Large-product wider applicability:** once the W10 concept is proven, the same functional principle should be adapted at other locations where recurring large/coarse-product loss and local geometry justify it, including the Sample Conveyors and relevant confined underground sections.
9. **Proposed summary addition:** include a management-facing list/matrix of all inspected conveyor sections and indicate where side skirting is:
   - already recommended;
   - likely suitable for later adaptation if the pilot succeeds;
   - or should be monitored/reviewed for possible future implementation.

## Important Scope Distinction

The two rollout philosophies are related but not identical:

- **Fines / cleaner-and-containment:** a system-wide functional standard after successful CV09 demonstration, applied through systematic review of relevant head/discharge locations across the inspected conveyor ecosystem.
- **Coarse / large-product side skirting:** a location-specific containment principle demonstrated at W10 and then adapted only where comparable loss mechanisms and local geometry confirm suitability.

The final summary must not imply universal side-skirt installation at every conveyor.
