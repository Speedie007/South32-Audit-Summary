## Contextual Interpretation

The fines-control rollout should be stated **explicitly and strongly** in the summary.

The intended programme is not merely to repair CV09 and then optionally consider similar improvements elsewhere.

The governing logic is:

1. fines accumulation is present throughout the inspected conveyor ecosystem;
2. effective fines control therefore requires each relevant conveyor head/discharge location to provide an effective cleaning-and-containment function;
3. CV09 is the pilot/reference location for proving the preferred upgraded cleaner philosophy;
4. the preferred normal arrangement is a **two-stage cleaner/scraper system** where verified site geometry permits;
5. the cleaner arrangement should use **spring-loaded/self-tensioning wear compensation** to maintain positive, controlled cleaner-to-belt contact as the cleaner elements wear;
6. the cleaner stages should operate while the belt is positively supported by the head pulley and should retain cleaner-removed fines within the intended head/discharge-chute process route;
7. once CV09 proves the required performance, the same functional philosophy should be systematically rolled out across the relevant underground and surface conveyor head/discharge locations;
8. where a specific chute or installation geometry genuinely cannot accommodate two effective stages, a **single effective pulley-supported, self-tensioning cleaner stage** may be used as the constrained minimum;
9. that one-stage arrangement should be treated as an exception justified by verified local constraints, not the normal target condition.

## Source-Alignment Boundary

The issued detailed report deliberately states that the **function is to be standardised**, rather than requiring physically identical hardware at every conveyor.

Accordingly, the summary should not imply that every discharge chute must receive an identical fabricated CV09 arrangement.

Instead, the summary should communicate that the preferred reference concept is:

**two effective cleaning stages + spring-loaded/self-tensioning wear compensation + pulley-supported cleaning + retention of removed fines within the discharge process**

with local adaptation permitted only where verified geometry, operating duty, maintenance access or other responsible-design constraints require it.

Where two effective stages cannot be accommodated, one effective self-tensioning stage is the constrained minimum.

## Contextual Significance

This point should be more explicit in the summary than it apparently was understood from the detailed report.

The intended management message is:

> **CV09 is the proof-of-concept location, not the end-state. Successful demonstration at CV09 should trigger a systematic underground-and-surface head/discharge upgrade programme based on the same cleaner-and-containment functional standard.**

The rollout should therefore be presented as the expected next programme step after successful pilot verification, not as an optional future consideration.

Detailed cleaner selection, exact spring forces, mounting, supports, dimensions, chute interfaces, guarding and other implementation details remain subject to verified site geometry and OEM/responsible detailed design.
