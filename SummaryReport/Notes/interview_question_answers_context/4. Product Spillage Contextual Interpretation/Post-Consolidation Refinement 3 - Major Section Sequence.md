## Accepted V4 Section Sequence

The three major technical sections are reordered as follows:

1. **Section 3 — Product / Material Spillage**
2. **Section 4 — Cable Belt Sheaves**
3. **Section 5 — Main Shaft Head Sheaves**

This supersedes the V3 order:

1. Section 3 — Main Shaft Head Sheaves
2. Section 4 — Cable Belt Sheaves
3. Section 5 — Product / Material Spillage

## Change-Control Rule

The technical content of each of the three sections is retained intact from V3.

Only the following changes are made to implement the new sequence:

- move the complete Product / Material Spillage section from Section 5 to Section 3;
- renumber its internal subsections from 5.1–5.x to 3.1–3.x;
- retain the complete Cable Belt Sheaves section as Section 4;
- move the complete Main Shaft Head Sheaves section from Section 3 to Section 5;
- update the draft version header to V4.

No technical rewriting, finding changes, recommendation changes or matrix changes are introduced as part of this sequencing refinement.

## Version-Control Effect

- V1 remains unchanged.
- V2 remains unchanged.
- V3 remains unchanged.
- V4 becomes the active review draft.
