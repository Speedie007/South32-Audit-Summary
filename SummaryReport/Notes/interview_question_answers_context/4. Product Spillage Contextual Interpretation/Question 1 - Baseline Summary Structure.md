## Contextual Interpretation

The Product Spillage section should use the proposed three-level structure as its **baseline summary architecture**.

### Level 1 — Inspection Coverage / System Overview

Provide a concise list or matrix of the underground and surface conveyor/process sections included in the inspection so the reader can see the breadth of the audit before moving into interpretation.

### Level 2 — Two Main Management Narratives

#### A. Fines / Carryback

Treat fines/carryback as the principal system-wide spillage narrative:

**system-wide fines presence → CV09 cleaner-and-containment proof of concept → demonstrate functional success → systematically review relevant underground and surface head/discharge locations → progressively implement/adapt the functional standard where applicable**

#### B. Coarse / Large-Product Loss

Treat larger/coarse product escape as the principal localized lateral-containment narrative:

**localized coarse/mixed-product loss → W10 side-skirt/spill-seal proof of concept → demonstrate effective containment → adapt/apply at Sample Conveyors and other locations where recurring lateral product loss and local geometry justify it**

The coarse-product narrative must remain location-specific rather than being presented as a universal system-wide defect.

### Level 3 — Supporting Local Workstreams

Other technically important findings remain visible but should receive shorter treatment so they do not compete with the two dominant spillage-control stories.

These include:

- Screens process-water containment and structural condition;
- L1 structural / belt-clearance condition;
- U4 and L3 tracking/alignment concerns;
- trip/restart product-handling controls;
- housekeeping, recovery and reintroduction controls.
