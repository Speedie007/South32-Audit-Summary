## Accepted Refinement

Working Draft V5 uses V4 as its baseline.

Section 3.2 is expanded to provide explicit fines/scraper rollout planning through:

- **3.2.1 Underground Sections — Proposed Rollout**
- **3.2.2 Surface Handling Sections — Proposed Rollout**
- **3.2.3 Surface Loadout Sections — Proposed Rollout**

The following distinctions govern the revised section:

- CV09 is the proof-of-concept location for the improved cleaner-and-containment standard.
- CV06 is an early cleaner-restoration and verification case, not a second pilot.
- CV05, CV07, CV08, CV10, CV11, CV12 and CV13 are **site-identified rollout locations**, not original controlled inspection groups from the issued Product Spillage audit.
- Surface Loadout rollout locations are L1, L2, L3, L4 and Sample Conveyors 1, 2 and 3.
- The Cable Belt Conveyor is retained from the user's rollout amendment as a site-identified underground rollout location.
- U4 remains a geometry-specific exception requiring a locally adapted cleaner arrangement.
- The scraper/cleaner programme and skirting programme remain deliberately separate:
  - scraper/cleaner control = system-wide review and progressive rollout at relevant discharge points;
  - side skirting = selective implementation only where coarse-product loss and local geometry justify it.
