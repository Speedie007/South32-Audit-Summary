# Interview Phase Plan

## Purpose

This file defines the staged interview structure for development of the South32 summary report context.

## Phase 1 — Report Contextual Foundation

Questions 1–N establish the foundational background context for the report: the proverbial **who, what, where, why, how**, together with scope, audience, intent, structure, interpretation rules, and expected reader outcome.

This phase remains open until enough background context has been established to support later technical interpretation without ambiguity.

When complete, Questions 1–N will be consolidated into:

`Report Contextual Foundation - Background Context Consolidation.md`

That consolidation becomes the authoritative background context for subsequent technical interview rounds.

## Phase 2 — Section-Specific Contextual Interpretation

After the foundation is closed, separate contextual interview rounds will be conducted for:

### A. Main Shaft Head Sheaves
The interview will establish contextual meaning of findings, interpretation, implications, remedial actions, remedial philosophy, sequencing, emphasis, and what should remain in the detailed source report.

### B. Cable Belt Sheaves
The interview will establish collective system-level interpretation, relationships between inspected sheave groups, implications, remedial actions, remedial philosophy, implementation order, and forward strategy.

### C. Product Spillage
The interview will establish contextual interpretation of the spillage findings, root-cause or contributing-cause logic, remedial actions, priority areas, dependencies, remediation sequence, and the holistic spillage-control philosophy.

## Phase 3 — Holistic Remedial Integration

After the three section-specific rounds are complete, a final synthesis round will establish:

- how the three component sets should be presented together;
- cross-cutting themes;
- overall remedial philosophy;
- sequencing across workstreams where relevant;
- dependencies;
- strategic implications;
- the final report narrative and section order;
- final conclusion and recommendations philosophy.

## Context Preservation Rule

Every interview question remains governed by the standard question-context procedure:

1. one file per question;
2. exact question;
3. user's answer;
4. contextual interpretation;
5. contextual significance;
6. round-level consolidation after each interview round;
7. final consolidation into the appropriate higher-level context.

This structure preserves historical context, reduces drift, and maintains traceability from final report language back to the interview and source reports.
