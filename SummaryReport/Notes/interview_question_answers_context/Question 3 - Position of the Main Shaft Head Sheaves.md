## Contextual Interpretation

The audit should be treated as comprising **three distinct technical component sets**:

1. **Main Shaft Head Sheaves** — a separate audited component associated with the main shaft lift system.
2. **Cable Belt Sheaves** — the group of sheaves functioning within the cable belt conveyor transport system.
3. **Product Spillage** — the investigation into product escaping from the conveyor transport system.

The Main Shaft Head Sheaves should therefore **not be absorbed into the Cable Belt Sheaves section** merely because both involve sheaves. They belong to a different operating system and require their own high-level treatment in the summary report.

The three components may be brought together under the same overall audit narrative, but their technical identities and remediation philosophies should remain distinguishable unless later evidence establishes meaningful relationships between them.

## Contextual Significance

This answer corrects the earlier provisional two-part framing.

The accepted report architecture should now start from **three audited areas**, not two:

**Main Shaft Head Sheaves + Cable Belt Sheaves + Product Spillage.**
