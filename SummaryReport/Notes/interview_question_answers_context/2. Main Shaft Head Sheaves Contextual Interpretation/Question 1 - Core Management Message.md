## Contextual Interpretation

The proposed management-level interpretation is accepted.

The Main Shaft Head Sheaves section should remain concise and should communicate that:

- the inspected sheaves were found to be in acceptable/serviceable condition;
- the recorded groove wear does not presently indicate a need for repair or replacement;
- no immediate remedial intervention is required based on the inspection findings;
- the inspection establishes a useful baseline for future comparison and wear trending;
- continued six-monthly inspection/monitoring is the appropriate forward action;
- formalizing the approved OEM/client groove-wear acceptance criterion is more relevant than corrective work at this stage.

The section is intentionally simple and does not require extensive interpretive expansion unless later evidence introduces a specific issue that adds value to the summary.

## Contextual Significance

This answer establishes the governing tone and depth for the Main Shaft Head Sheaves section:

**Acceptable present condition + no current remedial work + monitor and trend against a formalized criterion.**

The section should not be overdeveloped merely for symmetry with more complex audit areas.
