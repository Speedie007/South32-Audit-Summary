>
> Or would you prefer to leave that level of detail in the dedicated inspection report and keep the summary even simpler?

## User Answer

> prefer to leave that level of detail in the dedicated inspection report and keep the summary even simpler

## Contextual Interpretation

The summary report should **not** include the detailed governance point concerning formalization of the approved OEM/client groove-wear acceptance criterion.

That detail should remain in the dedicated Main Shaft Head Sheaves inspection report.

The summary section should stay focused on the management-level outcome:

- the three inspected sheaves were found acceptable/serviceable;
- the recorded groove wear is minor;
- no repair or replacement is presently required;
- routine six-monthly monitoring should continue.

## Contextual Significance

This further confirms that the Main Shaft Head Sheaves section should remain deliberately concise.

The summary should avoid importing secondary inspection-control detail where it does not materially improve the high-level message.
