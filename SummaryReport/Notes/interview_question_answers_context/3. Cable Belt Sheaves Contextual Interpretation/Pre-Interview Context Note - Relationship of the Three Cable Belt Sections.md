# Pre-Interview Context Note - Relationship of the Three Cable Belt Sections

## Status

Accepted contextual clarification provided before Question 1.

## User Context

## Contextual Interpretation

The three Cable Belt reports cover physically different sections of the same broader Cable Belt infrastructure:

1. **Cable Belt Main Drive Sheaves**
2. **Upper Gantry Guide Sheaves**
3. **Cable Belt Lower Angle Station Sheaves**

Each section performs a different guiding/supporting function as the cable belt rope passes through the system.

They should therefore be understood and presented within one broader **Cable Belt Sheaves / Cable Belt infrastructure** context, while still preserving the local condition, findings, and recommendations applicable to each physical section.

This relationship does not mean that every local defect or condition identified in one section automatically applies to the other sections. Any cross-section causal or system-level conclusion must remain supported by the combined inspection evidence and the accepted contextual interpretation.

## Contextual Significance

This establishes the structural basis for the Round 3 interview:

- the three reports are not unrelated standalone assets;
- they are separate inspected sections of one Cable Belt system;
- the summary may legitimately synthesize them at system level;
- section-specific differences must remain visible where technically important;
- system-level interpretation must not erase or fabricate local distinctions.
