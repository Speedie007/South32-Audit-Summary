## Contextual Interpretation

The Main Drive Sheaves should be treated as an **independent subsection** within the broader Cable Belt narrative.

Although the Main Drive and Angle Station sections show related wear mechanisms, they should not be merged into one causal explanation.

The Main Drive emphasis should be:

- physical alignment and rope-path geometry through the Drive Wheels and Deflector-Guide Sheaves;
- asymmetric liner/groove deterioration as evidence consistent with off-centre or laterally loaded rope travel;
- the need for a formal geometry/alignment survey to quantify the actual condition before defining final corrective repositioning;

## Contextual Significance

The final Cable Belt section should therefore preserve two distinct but related narratives:

1. **Angle Station:** a coupled Upper/Lower Angle Station rope-path mechanism with observed non-zero fleet angle, asymmetric groove wear, accelerated rope wear and bearing-loading consequences.

This separation prevents over-generalization while still allowing the final report to identify a broader Cable Belt theme: incorrect rope-path geometry can drive asymmetric rope-contact wear and adversely affect rope/component life.
