**measure the actual alignment / rope-path geometry → define the required correction → correct the relevant sheave / guide positions → renew the worn Drive Wheel liners and Deflector-Guide Sheaves as one coordinated intervention → verify centred tracking and establish a new baseline**

- why geometry must be measured before final correction is defined;
- why geometry correction and renewal of worn rope-contact components are interdependent;
- why simply replacing worn components without correcting the confirmed geometry could reproduce the wear mechanism;
- why post-intervention verification and baseline establishment are necessary.

It should **not** restate the Main Drive report as a maintenance work instruction.

Where additional detail is required, the reader should be directed to the dedicated Main Drive inspection report.
