The Upper Gantry Guide Sheaves subsection should remain concise and management-level.

- all four Upper Gantry Guide Sheaves remain serviceable within the inspection scope;
- no immediate remedial intervention is required;
- the rear sheaves are generally in good condition;
- the front sheaves show groove-base scoring and therefore warrant closer initial monitoring;
- subsequent inspections should trend groove depth, groove profile and visible scoring against the current inspection baseline.

Detailed dimensional readings, exact inspection intervals, individual asset data, photographs, acceptance references and other granular inspection evidence should remain in the dedicated Upper Gantry Guide Sheaves report.

- the section is presently serviceable;
- no major corrective intervention is indicated;
