They represent contextual understanding developed during the audit through site conditions, observations, and discussions with personnel.

### 1. Main Shaft Head Sheaves

- a scheduled or periodic inspection requirement; and
- regulatory and/or safety inspection requirements.

This aligns with the previously established context that the Main Shaft Head Sheave assessment formed part of the mine's periodic safety-related inspection regime.

### 2. Cable Belt Sheaves

## Contextual Significance

This answer establishes an important reporting rule:

**The summary report must distinguish between formally documented scope/instructions and contextual understanding developed during the audit.**

Where the original appointment or source documentation does not explicitly state the commissioning reason, the report should use qualified language such as:

- "The inspection was understood to form part of..."
- "Site context indicated..."
- "Discussions and observations during the audit suggested..."
- "The assessment appears to have been undertaken in the context of..."

The report should avoid wording such as "South32 commissioned ISS because..." unless that statement is explicitly supported by source documentation.
