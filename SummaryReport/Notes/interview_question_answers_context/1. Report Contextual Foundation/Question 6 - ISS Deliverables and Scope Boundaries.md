## Contextual Interpretation

ISS's role varied by audit area and should not be described as one uniform scope across all three sections.

### 1. Main Shaft Head Sheaves

ISS was expected primarily to:

- inspect and record the condition of the Main Shaft Head Sheaves; and
- advise whether the equipment could remain in service.

This was therefore primarily a condition-assessment and serviceability-opinion scope.

### 2. Cable Belt Sheaves

The Cable Belt Sheave scope was broader and combined several forms of technical evaluation with differing levels of emphasis.

ISS's role included:

- inspecting and recording condition;
- diagnosing probable causes;
- assessing severity and/or risk;
- recommending remedial actions;
- defining repair methodology at an appropriate conceptual or advisory level;
- advising whether equipment could remain in service; and
- providing an independent technical opinion.

The final summary report should therefore treat the cable belt sheave work as more than a simple inspection record. It included condition interpretation, probable-cause analysis, serviceability consideration, and remedial guidance.

### 3. Product Spillage

ISS's role included:

- inspecting and recording the condition or circumstances associated with the spillage;
- diagnosing probable causes;
- recommending remedial actions;
- defining repair/remediation methodology at an appropriate conceptual or advisory level; and
- providing an independent technical opinion.

This was therefore an investigative and remedial-advisory scope rather than a detailed engineering-design scope.

## Scope Exclusions

Across all three audit areas, ISS was **not expected to provide**:

- detailed engineering design;
- final repair drawings;
- issued-for-construction drawings;
- implementation supervision.

Where conceptual technical drawings appear in the source reports, they were included only to support explanation of findings or recommendations.

These drawings:

- were conceptual;
- were not to scale;
- were not intended as final technical specifications;
- were not intended as construction or fabrication drawings;
- were used to improve visual understanding where graphical explanation was clearer than verbal description alone.

## Contextual Significance

This answer establishes an important distinction between:

- **inspection and engineering advisory output**, and
- **detailed engineering design deliverables**.

The summary report should preserve that distinction.

Where it refers to repair methodology or conceptual drawings, it should make clear that ISS was providing **technical guidance and conceptual remediation direction**, not a final engineered design package.

This is particularly important for procurement, project, and maintenance readers who might otherwise interpret conceptual sketches or repair concepts as implementation-ready specifications.
